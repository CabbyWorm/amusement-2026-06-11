How to play

Every language in this game is invented the moment the round begins — its sounds, suffixes, word order, even vowel harmony are rolled from a seed. It exists in no dictionary, no phrasebook, no corpus on Earth. Only here.

1. Read the field corpus: genuine sentences with their translations.
2. Crack the system: find the stems, isolate the suffixes, deduce the order.
3. Translate sentences you've never seen — in both directions — by tapping tiles.

Fairness guarantee: every stem and every suffix you need is attested somewhere in the corpus. There is no guessing here, only deduction.

Tip: tap any foreign word to light up its exact twins (bright) and likely relatives (faint). Long journeys deserve a notebook — it's the 📓 up top.

For the brave: past level 5 the rails enter the bonus tiers — ergative case, prefixes, sandhi, reduplicated plurals, dual number, noun classes — with score multipliers up to ×10. Same promise holds: everything you need is in the corpus.
